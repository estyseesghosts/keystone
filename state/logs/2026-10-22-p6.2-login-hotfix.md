## Decided

- Akkoma: keeping `write:conversations` follows the slice-1.5 `write:reports`
  precedent (Akkoma checks umbrella scopes and already tolerated granular adds
  with no login breakage). No Akkoma special-casing added.

  "carries no LATER scope" test); the store `scopeHealth` test
