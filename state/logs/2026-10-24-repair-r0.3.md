## Decided

- KAV ownership: the **sheet** (`Compose.tsx`) is the single offset owner; the composer's inner KAV is skipped on that path only. Rationale: the sheet already owns all other keyboard geometry (`sheetVisibleMaxHeight` cap, Android `bottomPad`, safe-area inset), and keeping the KAV as the ancestor of the sheet `ScrollView` preserves the standard offset pattern instead of a KAV nested inside scroll content. `Home.tsx` standalone behavior is byte-identical (default prop). No ADR (choice is within the slice's two sanctioned options, no model/config consequence).
