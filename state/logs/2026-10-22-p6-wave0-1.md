## Decided

Recorded in `docs/DECISIONS.md` under 2026-10-22 (P6.0.x / P6.1.x) and
`docs/adr/0003-oauth-scopes-now-vs-later.md`. Load-bearing choices worth
noting here: WebSocket auth by subprotocol; search cursors only from
Link; filter groups are the unit, not one keyword; translation evidence
is family-scoped; scopes split now/later with no revocation; Misskey
import is upload-then-fileId and export is enqueue-only; `folderId`
optional/root-omitted; like support is probed, never flavor-seeded;
Misskey permission errors surface globally; DMs are an explicit
client-gap capability; tag mute does not exist upstream; lists/poll
fields are per-flavor capability-gated; Misskey channels are first-class
(product + stream + scopes); reports send only chosen fields; avatar/
banner go through a family-specific upload seam; announcements use the
endpoints that actually exist (`i/read-announcement`, not a dismiss
endpoint).
