  Pinetree), emoji popup inserting at the caret, CW toggle, scheduling

  contract on Misskey/Sharkey/Pinetree — checked Misskey `develop`
  endpoint sources); local-only kept. No GIF/location buttons.
  Compact mode for the detail reply box: one avatar + `Post your reply`
  button → `Replying to @user` + autofocused editor → collapses on
  post. Post body building, limits, and error-state preservation
  untouched.
- `frontend/src/routes/Note.tsx`: reply composer uses compact mode
  with the target handle.
- `frontend/src/components/icons.tsx`: `MediaIcon`, `PollIcon`,
  `ScheduleIcon` (same stroke set).
- `frontend/src/styles/global.css`: composer-main/audience/toolbtn/
  emoji-wrap/compact-row styles; explicit pressed-toggle rule after the
  bare-icon base (specificity tie fix).
