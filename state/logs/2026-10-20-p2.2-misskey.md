## Decided (→ DECISIONS.md transcription by orchestrator)

- `call: WireCall` FIRST param on all submodule functions (TS required-after-optional rule; mirrors 2.1 store-first).
- `downloadJson` (DOM anchor-click) replaced by pure `serializeExport` — UI owns download/share (RN has no anchor download).
- Stream/external-account split deleted: per-session pools carry their own origin+token; no global active-session lookups in the data layer.
- `createMisskeyBackend` requires a transport; `stream()` null without `origin`; Pinetree `supported: false`.
- `mfm-js@0.27.0` added to root deps (translate needs it now; 2.4 reuses it); `eventemitter3` + `mfm-js` also declared in `packages/fedi/package.json` (fedi is not a pnpm workspace member — root hoisting serves resolution).
- Barrel collision (below) needs an orchestrator call: per-family namespacing of the fedi barrel.
