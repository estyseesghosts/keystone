Decided (→ DECISIONS/ADR only if the owner promotes them):

- The badge is a prop, not a store read in `badges.ts` (AGENTS §6
  purity). The live card owns the per-session store read; the pure
  module stays trivially testable and reusable.
- One predicate serves filter + badge (`isSoftMutedPost`). Divergent
  copies were the real risk: a row the feed drops and a row the card
  badges must be decided by the same rule.
- Judge the RENDERED author (`appearNoteOf`), not the wrapper. A pure
  boost whose original is muted is badged; a boost whose reposter is
  muted but whose original is not is left unbadged — the visible author
  is not muted, and feeds hide the wrapper anyway (news only, no
  invented signal).
- Placement: after `filtered` (moderation-ish group), before
  `reactions` (capability marker). Pinned to this order by tests.
- Label `Soft-muted`, one new Lingui message. Locale catalogs were NOT
  re-extracted/compiled: the coordinator compiles after merge.
