  *earlier* than render and flipped the exact-boundary `post.test` fixture
  (`22h ago` → `21h ago`). ms-at-subscribe matches old first-paint behavior
  deterministically and is still minute-exact for every label; notify fires on
  rollover only. Finding + fix recorded here, no ADR (no load-bearing shape change).
- `PostPoll` `useLingui()` → `useI18n()` (coordinator's X9.4 pattern; the component
  needs reactive `i18n.locale`; identical under provider, all poll tests green).
