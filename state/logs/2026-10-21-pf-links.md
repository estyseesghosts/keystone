## Decided

- Fix at the RichText fallback layer, not parser/callers (one layer): parser
  shapes proven correct against the rig; every handler-less surface is fixed
  by the default.
- href-acct beats a bare label: a server-provided profile URL is authoritative
  over display text (fixes remote bare-label + display-name labels); a full
  `user@host` label still wins outright. Proven safe: all three acct shapes
  resolve live on Akkoma.
- Port 8098 for expo web, not the assigned 8088 (8088 is held by a broken
  `keystonev2-wsF` instance; 8096 by pf-emoji; other agents own the rest).
