## Decided (→ ADR 0005)

- Unknown flavor → full set: a wrongly-narrowed Akkoma token breaks
  shipped features; stock Mastodon accepts the full set, so full is the
  safe default. Rare (login always detects first) and self-healing via
  re-detect on upgrade re-auth.
- No detect-first probe in LoginFlows: flavor is a caller-provided
  optional param; detection already happens upstream.
- Akkoma keeps the full granular+umbrella string (ADR 0003 probe stands).
